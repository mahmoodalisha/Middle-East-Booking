Middle East Booking - Cancellation Information

Does Middle East Booking currently provide a cancellation policy?
Yes. Users can receive a full refund if they cancel their booking at least 5 days before the booking date.

Can users cancel a booking?
Yes. Users can cancel their booking according to the cancellation policy.

Are refunds supported?
Yes. If the user cancels their booking at least 5 days before the booking date, a full refund will be provided.

How much refund will the user receive if they cancel 5 days before the booking date?
The user will receive a full refund.
