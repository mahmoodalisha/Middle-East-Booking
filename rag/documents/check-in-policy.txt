Middle East Booking - Check-in Information

What check-in time does the application use?
The application uses UTC (Coordinated Universal Time).

What check-out time does the application use?
The application uses UTC (Coordinated Universal Time).

Does the application support early check-in?
That depends on the specific hotel policy if the hotels can allow early check-ins.

Does the application support late checkout?
That depends on the specific hotel policy if the hotels can allow late check-outs.

What documents are required during check-in?
The documents that are required during check-in are your passport and your government national Id card.
